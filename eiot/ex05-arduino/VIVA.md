# Viva: ex05-arduino

**Q1. Two mandatory functions in a sketch?**
setup() runs once; loop() runs repeatedly.

**Q2. What does pinMode do?**
Configures a pin as INPUT, OUTPUT or INPUT_PULLUP.

**Q3. Which microcontroller is on the UNO?**
ATmega328P, 16 MHz, 5 V logic.

**Q4. What is delay(1000)?**
Blocks for 1000 ms.

**Q5. Role of the bootloader?**
Lets the board be programmed over USB without an external programmer.
