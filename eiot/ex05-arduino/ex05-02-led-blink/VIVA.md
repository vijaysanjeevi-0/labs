# Ex 5.2 viva — LED blink

1. **Two mandatory functions in a sketch?** `setup()` runs once; `loop()` runs repeatedly.
2. **What does `pinMode` do?** Configures a pin as `INPUT`, `OUTPUT` or `INPUT_PULLUP`.
3. **Which microcontroller is on the UNO?** ATmega328P, 16 MHz, 5 V logic.
4. **What does `delay(1000)` do?** Blocks execution for 1000 ms.
5. **Why must the sketch folder share the `.ino` file's name?** The Arduino IDE requires it to identify and open the sketch correctly.
