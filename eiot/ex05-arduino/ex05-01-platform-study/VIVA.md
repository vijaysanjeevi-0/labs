# Ex 5.1 viva — Arduino platform

1. **What is Arduino?** An open-source hardware/software platform: a microcontroller board plus a simplified C/C++ IDE for quick prototyping.
2. **What microcontroller is on the UNO?** ATmega328P, 16 MHz, 5 V logic.
3. **Digital vs analog pins on the UNO?** 14 digital I/O pins (6 PWM-capable) and 6 analog input pins (ADC).
4. **What is the bootloader?** Pre-loaded firmware that lets the board be programmed over USB without a separate hardware programmer.
5. **What is a shield?** A board that plugs directly onto the UNO's headers to add functionality (motor driver, Ethernet, etc.).
