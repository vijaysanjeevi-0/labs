# Viva: ex06-communication

**Q1. Bluetooth range and band?**
About 10 m (Class 2) in the 2.4 GHz ISM band.

**Q2. Zigbee standard and use?**
IEEE 802.15.4; low-power, low-rate mesh for sensors and home automation.

**Q3. Why SoftwareSerial?**
The hardware UART is used for the USB monitor, so another serial port is emulated on pins 2 and 3.

**Q4. Default HC-05 baud rate?**
9600 in data mode (38400 in AT mode).

**Q5. GSM vs Zigbee?**
GSM is wide-area cellular; Zigbee is short-range low-power mesh.
